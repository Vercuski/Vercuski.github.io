Who Am I?


Long time developer, primarily focused on the Backend and Database areas. My preferred Frontend frameworks are Vue.js and Microsoft Blazor.


The lion's share of my career has been spent working with the Microsoft stack (C# / .NET / .NET Core / Microsoft SQL Server), but I have experience with a variety of languages, frameworks, and databases.


I've held a number of positions in the span of my career:

  • Software Engineer (Junior/Mid/Senior/Lead/Principal)

I enjoy mentoring and teaching others, and helping them to grow in their careers. I am also a lifelong learner trying my best to keep my skills up to date. My PC is a mishmash of projects and technologies that I'm currently learning or working on.
