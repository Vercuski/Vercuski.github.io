PRIVACY


What this site collects, why, and who else handles it.


Last updated October 20, 2026

The short version

This is a personal website. It doesn't have accounts, it doesn't sell or share your information for advertising, and the only personal details it collects are the ones you choose to send through the contact form.

Contact form

When you send a message, the form collects your full name, email address, and message. These are used only to read and reply to your message.

Messages are delivered by Web3Forms, which passes them on to my personal email inbox. They are kept there for as long as they are useful to the conversation, and are not added to any mailing list.

Spam protection

The contact form uses hCaptcha to check that messages come from people rather than bots. hCaptcha loads only on the contact page and collects information about your browser and how you interact with the check. Its use is governed by the hCaptcha Privacy Policy and Terms of Service.

Analytics

The site uses Google Analytics to see which pages are visited and how people find the site. Google Analytics sets cookies and collects information such as the pages you view, your approximate location, and your browser and device type. I only look at this in aggregate. You can read how Google uses it in How Google uses information from sites that use its services, and opt out with the Google Analytics opt-out add-on or your browser's tracking protection.

Hosting

Like any website, the server that hosts this site may record standard request information, such as your IP address and browser, in its logs to keep the site running and secure.

Your choices

If you've sent a message and would like it deleted, or have any questions about this page, get in touch and I'll take care of it.
