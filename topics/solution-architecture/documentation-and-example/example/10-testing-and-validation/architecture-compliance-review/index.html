Architecture Compliance Review — Gate 2

Result:

Ready with conditions Tapline meets the Gate 2 success criteria. Every CRT validator accepts contactless EMV, and 99.987% of taps were processed correctly (March–May 2028) against a 99.95% target. The architecture is implemented as decided in ADR-0001 to ADR-0008, with one approved exception per edge case (EX-001, EX-002). Two Medium findings become conditions: finishing the Vantor firmware 7.4 rollout, and removing the last uses of TimeZoneInfo outside the domain. Both are due before the next clock change (2028-11-05). The Program Board accepted the result on 2028-06-28, and Gate 2 was passed on 2028-06-30.

1. Scope of Review

  • Gate: Phase 2 exit, Systemwide open payments (Roadmap §4).
  • Period of evidence: 2028-03-01 to 2028-05-31 for production data; test and drill evidence since Gate 1.
  • In scope:
    • All Tapline containers in production.
    • Validators on all six garages, both rail lines and the ferry.
    • The Rider App and portals, back office, and integrations with Northgate, DHS, CornerPay, ERP and the RiverCard Bridge.
    • Documentation set.
  • Out of scope: Phase 3 customer migration readiness, assessed at Gate 3; Phase 4 partner capabilities.
  • Method: the Governance Framework §3.3 checklist, the template items, and the time-handling check added after INC-2027-1107-01. Evidence comes from SLO dashboards, test reports, the ADR log, exception register, threat model, compliance matrix and risk register. Each owning team submitted a self-assessment, and the ARB sampled the evidence.
