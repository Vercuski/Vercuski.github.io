Project Tapline Brief

Functional Requirements Specification — Project Tapline

1. Overview

This specification turns the Business Requirements into testable system behavior. Every functional requirement (FR) traces to at least one business requirement (BR) and forward to at least one test case (TC) in the Test Strategy. Quality targets such as speed, availability and security are in the Non-Functional Requirements. Rider and staff journeys are in Use Cases / User Stories.

Conventions

  • IDs follow the pattern FR-<area>-nn. Areas: TAP (validation), FARE (fare calculation), CAP (capping), ACCT (accounts and media), PAY (payments and funding), ELIG (reduced fare), CS (customer service), INSP (inspection), POL (fare policy), FIN (finance), PART (partners), DEV (devices), MIG (RiverCard migration), DATA (reporting and privacy).
  • Priority uses MoSCoW. "Phase" is the roadmap phase in which the FR first goes live; see the Roadmap.
  • Media means anything a rider taps: a bank card, a phone wallet, a CRT card or an app ticket. Token means the media token, the identifier the system holds for a medium. For bank cards it is an irreversible keyed hash, never the card number. Terms are defined in the Domain Model.

2. Functional Requirements

2.1 Tap and validation (TAP)

IDRequirementSource (BR-ID)PriorityAcceptance Criteria
FR-TAP-01A validator accepts a tap from a contactless EMV bank card or phone wallet and shows accept or deny with a distinct tone and color.BR-01, BR-24Must · P1Valid card → green screen and accept tone; deny-listed card → red screen and deny tone with a reason code; result shown on every tap.
FR-TAP-02A validator accepts a tap from a CRT card and from an app ticket (QR code).BR-02, BR-03Must (card) / Should (QR) · P1CRT card and a valid app QR are accepted; an expired or tampered QR is denied.
FR-TAP-03A validator decides accept or deny locally, using its deny list and the medium's own checks, with no call to the back office during the tap.BR-07, BR-08Must · P0Accept/deny is identical with the network disconnected; see NFR-PERF-01 for timing.
FR-TAP-04A validator stores every tap, signs it, and uploads it when connected. A tap is deleted from the validator only after the back office confirms receipt.BR-08, BR-16Must · P0No taps are lost after 72 h offline followed by reconnection; duplicate uploads are ignored (each tap has a unique ID).
FR-TAP-05A validator ignores a repeat tap of the same medium within 10 seconds on the same validator ("double tap").BR-07Must · P1Second tap within 10 s → shows "already paid", no new tap recorded.
FR-TAP-06A validator receives deny-list updates (additions and removals) and applies them without a restart.BR-12, BR-13Must · P0A medium blocked in the back office is denied at a connected validator; see NFR-PERF-04 for timing.
FR-TAP-07A bank card not seen before is accepted on its first tap if it passes the card's own offline checks ("first-ride risk"). If its charge is later declined, it is added to the deny list until the debt is paid.BR-01, BR-16Must · P1Declined charge → card on deny list within one deny-list cycle; paying the debt in the portal removes it.

2.2 Fare calculation (FARE)

IDRequirementSource (BR-ID)PriorityAcceptance Criteria
FR-FARE-01The system groups taps by the same medium into trips, applying the transfer rule of the operator where the trip began.BR-05Must · P1Taps on Route 12 at 08:00 and Blue Line at 09:30 → one trip, one $2.00 fare.
FR-FARE-02One fare covers unlimited CRT transfers for 2 hours from the first tap. A tap after 2 hours starts a new fare.BR-05Must · P1Taps at 08:00 and 10:00:59 → 2 fares; taps at 08:00 and 09:59:59 → 1 fare.
FR-FARE-03Each tap is assigned to a service day running from 03:00 to 02:59 local time. Taps between 00:00 and 02:59 belong to the previous calendar day's service day.BR-04Must · P1Tap at 01:45 on Saturday counts toward Friday's service day.
FR-FARE-04Fare calculation uses the fare rules in effect at the time of the tap, not at the time of processing.BR-15, BR-16Must · P1A tap made before a fare change but uploaded after it is charged the old fare.
FR-FARE-05Riders with reduced-fare status are charged the reduced fare for every tap from any of their linked media.BR-06Must · P2Linked bank card on a reduced-fare account → $1.00 fares.
FR-FARE-06Fare calculation can be repeated for any past period and produces the same result (deterministic).BR-13, BR-16Must · P1Re-running a service day's taps gives identical charges to the cent.

2.3 Fare capping (CAP)

IDRequirementSource (BR-ID)PriorityAcceptance Criteria
FR-CAP-01When a rider's fares in a service day reach the daily cap, further taps that service day are free.BR-04Must · P1Three $2.00 taps → $2.00, $2.00, $1.00, then $0.00 for every later tap that service day.
FR-CAP-02When a rider's fares in a calendar week (Monday 03:00 to the following Monday 02:59) reach the weekly cap, further taps that week are free.BR-04Must · P1After $25.00 in fares in a week, every tap is $0.00 until Monday 03:00.
FR-CAP-03Caps are counted per account across all linked media.BR-04, BR-10Must · P2Two $2.00 taps on a bank card and one on a CRT card in the same account → total $5.00 for the day, not $6.00.
FR-CAP-04Reduced-fare accounts use the reduced caps ($2.50 daily, $12.50 weekly).BR-04, BR-06Must · P2Reduced-fare rider charged $1.00, $1.00, $0.50, then $0.00.
FR-CAP-05A bank card that is not linked to an account is capped on its own.BR-04Must · P1Unregistered card reaches the daily cap like any account.
FR-CAP-06The app and portal show progress toward the daily and weekly caps.BR-04, BR-10Should · P2Cap progress shown within the time in NFR-PERF-03.

2.4 Accounts and media (ACCT)

IDRequirementSource (BR-ID)PriorityAcceptance Criteria
FR-ACCT-01A rider can create an account with an email address or mobile number, verified by a one-time code.BR-10Must · P1Account active after verification; duplicate email or number rejected.
FR-ACCT-02A rider can link up to 5 media to an account: bank cards, phone wallets and CRT cards.BR-10Must · P1Linking a bank card needs a $0.00 verification with Northgate; the 6th medium is rejected with a message.
FR-ACCT-03A rider can see every tap, trip and charge for the last 13 months and download a statement (PDF or CSV).BR-10Must · P1Statement totals equal the charges on the bank statement for the same period.
FR-ACCT-04A rider can block a lost or stolen medium and move the balance, cap progress and reduced-fare status to a replacement.BR-12Must · P2Blocked medium denied at validators (FR-TAP-06); the replacement shows the same balance and cap progress.
FR-ACCT-05A rider can see past taps from a bank card used before the account existed, by linking that card.BR-10Should · P2Linking a card shows its last 90 days of trips.
FR-ACCT-06A CRT card can be issued at retail locations and used immediately without an account.BR-02Must · P2New card accepted at a validator within 15 minutes of a cash load at retail.

2.5 Payments and funding (PAY)

IDRequirementSource (BR-ID)PriorityAcceptance Criteria
FR-PAY-01Fares on bank cards and wallets are charged to the card in combined daily charges through Northgate, not one charge per tap.BR-01, BR-04Must · P1One charge per card per service day, equal to the sum of that day's fares after capping.
FR-PAY-02A rider can add stored value to an account with a card online, from $5 to $100.BR-11Must · P2Balance updated within 1 minute of a successful payment.
FR-PAY-03A rider can set automatic top-up: when the balance falls below a threshold, a set amount is charged to a chosen card.BR-11Must · P2Top-up triggered once per threshold crossing; a failed top-up notifies the rider.
FR-PAY-04A rider can add cash to a CRT card or account at any CornerPay location.BR-02, BR-11Must · P2Retail load posted to the account within 15 minutes.
FR-PAY-05A rider with an outstanding debt from a declined charge can pay it in the app or portal.BR-13Must · P1Paying the debt removes the card from the deny list (FR-TAP-07).

2.6 Reduced fare (ELIG)

IDRequirementSource (BR-ID)PriorityAcceptance Criteria
FR-ELIG-01A rider can apply online for low-income reduced fare. Eligibility is confirmed through the State DHS using the rider's consent and case number.BR-06Must · P2Matching DHS record → reduced fare active the same day; no match → application sent to manual review.
FR-ELIG-02Customer service can approve senior and disability reduced fares from documents, with the documents stored for 90 days and then deleted.BR-06, BR-26Must · P2Approved status applies to all linked media; documents purged after 90 days.
FR-ELIG-03Reduced-fare status has an expiry date. The rider is reminded 30 days before it expires and can renew.BR-06Must · P2Reminder sent 30 days before; full fare applies the day after expiry if not renewed.

2.7 Customer service and refunds (CS)

IDRequirementSource (BR-ID)PriorityAcceptance Criteria
FR-CS-01Customer service can find an account by email, phone, CRT card number, or the last 4 digits and expiry of a bank card.BR-13, BR-23Must · P1Search returns matches without ever showing or storing the full card number.
FR-CS-02Customer service can issue a refund or fare adjustment. Adjustments over $50, or more than 3 for the same account in 30 days, need a second approver.BR-13Must · P1Over-limit adjustment held until approved by a second user; every adjustment logged with the user and reason.
FR-CS-03A rider can dispute a charge in the app or portal and track its status.BR-13Should · P2Dispute visible to customer service within 1 minute; rider notified of the outcome.

2.8 Inspection (INSP)

IDRequirementSource (BR-ID)PriorityAcceptance Criteria
FR-INSP-01A fare inspector can tap a rider's medium on a handheld device and see whether it has a valid tap for the current trip.BR-09Must · P1Valid tap within the transfer window → "paid"; otherwise "no valid fare". Works offline using taps synced to the device in the last 5 minutes.
FR-INSP-02The handheld device shows no personal information: no name, no trip history, and no card number.BR-09, BR-26Must · P1Screen shows only paid/not paid, fare type (full or reduced) and the time of the last tap.

2.9 Fare policy (POL)

IDRequirementSource (BR-ID)PriorityAcceptance Criteria
FR-POL-01Authorized fare-policy staff can create a new version of fare rules (prices, caps, transfer window, products) with a future effective date and time, with no software release.BR-15Must · P1New version takes effect at the stated time; earlier taps use the earlier version (FR-FARE-04).
FR-POL-02A fare rule version must be approved by a second authorized user before it can take effect.BR-15, BR-16Must · P1Unapproved version cannot be scheduled.
FR-POL-03Before approval, a fare rule version can be simulated against a past week of real taps, showing the revenue and rider impact.BR-15, BR-19Should · P2Simulation report shows revenue difference and number of riders affected by fare category.
FR-POL-04Each operator manages its own fare rules. One operator cannot see or change another's.BR-15Must · P4HCT user cannot open CRT's fare rules.

2.10 Finance and reconciliation (FIN)

IDRequirementSource (BR-ID)PriorityAcceptance Criteria
FR-FIN-01Every charge, payment, refund, adjustment and balance movement is recorded as a permanent ledger entry. Entries are never changed or deleted; corrections are new entries.BR-16Must · P0No update or delete path exists for ledger entries; a correction shows both entries.
FR-FIN-02Each day the system reconciles fares charged against Northgate's settlement report and flags any difference.BR-16Must · P1Daily reconciliation report; any difference over $0.00 raises an exception for Revenue Operations.
FR-FIN-03Each day the system posts summarized journal entries to CRT's ERP general ledger.BR-16Must · P1GL totals match the ledger for the day.
FR-FIN-04The system reports outstanding stored value (customer liability) per day, by media type.BR-16, BR-21Must · P1Liability total equals the sum of all account and card balances.
FR-FIN-05Any fare can be traced from the tap to the charge, settlement batch, bank deposit and GL entry.BR-16Must · P1A sample of 100 random taps traces end to end in an audit test.

2.11 Partners (PART)

IDRequirementSource (BR-ID)PriorityAcceptance Criteria
FR-PART-01The system calculates each operator's share of revenue daily, according to the apportionment rules in the regional agreement.BR-17Must · P4Shares for a service day sum exactly to the revenue collected.
FR-PART-02Each operator can download a daily apportionment statement with trip-level detail (no rider identity).BR-17, BR-26Must · P4Statement available by 08:00 for the previous service day.
FR-PART-03Microtransit trips booked through Keel Mobility are charged to the rider's account and count toward caps.BR-20Could · P4A Keel trip charge appears in the account and counts toward the daily cap.

2.12 Devices (DEV)

IDRequirementSource (BR-ID)PriorityAcceptance Criteria
FR-DEV-01Operations staff can see every validator's status (online, offline, faulty), location, last contact and software version.BR-18Should · P1Dashboard shows a validator offline within 10 minutes of its last missed heartbeat.
FR-DEV-02Software and configuration updates can be sent to validators in groups, and stopped if problems appear.BR-18, BR-08Must · P1An update to a test group of 20 validators does not reach other groups until promoted.

2.13 RiverCard migration (MIG)

IDRequirementSource (BR-ID)PriorityAcceptance Criteria
FR-MIG-01Tapline validators accept RiverCard cards until the RiverCard acceptance end date, deducting fares from the RiverCard balance.BR-22Must · P3RiverCard tap on a Tapline validator deducts the correct fare from the RiverCard balance.
FR-MIG-02A RiverCard holder can move their balance, reduced-fare status and automatic top-up to a Tapline account, online or at a customer service center.BR-21Must · P3Balance appears in Tapline to the cent; the RiverCard is deactivated.
FR-MIG-03A RiverCard holder can request a refund of their balance instead of moving it.BR-21Must · P3Refund paid by check or bank transfer within 30 days.
FR-MIG-04Unclaimed RiverCard balances are reported for transfer under state unclaimed-property law.BR-21Must · P3Report lists every unclaimed balance and totals to the remaining liability.

2.14 Reporting and privacy (DATA)

IDRequirementSource (BR-ID)PriorityAcceptance Criteria
FR-DATA-01Fare usage reports by route, stop, time and census tract are produced from de-identified data.BR-19Must · P2Report data contains no account IDs or tokens; groups of fewer than 10 riders are suppressed.
FR-DATA-02A rider can choose to have their trip history deleted after 90 days instead of 13 months.BR-14, BR-26Must · P2Trips older than 90 days not shown and not retrievable for that account; financial totals kept.
FR-DATA-03All rider-facing screens, emails and notifications are available in English, Spanish, Vietnamese and Simplified Chinese.BR-25Must · P1Language can be changed on every screen; all four languages pass content review.
