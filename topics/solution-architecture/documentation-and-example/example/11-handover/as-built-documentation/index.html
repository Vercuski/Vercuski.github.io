As-Built Documentation — Project Tapline (Phase 2)

What is actually running, July 2028

This records Tapline as deployed at the end of Phase 2. It is the handover from the program's delivery team to CRT's permanent product teams and IT Operations, as the delivery partner rolls off in August 2028. The design held. The account-based model, offline-first edge, two-stream pipeline, card-data zone and append-only ledger are all as planned. There are 14 deviations (§3). Most came from the pilot, Castellan's delays and the fall-back incident. Where this document and the SAD (v1.0, October 2026) disagree, this document describes reality. SAD v1.1 is due before the Gate 3 design review (finding F-6).

1. Summary

Deployed:

  • About 4,800 validators: 4,680 Vantor V7 on all six garages and both rail lines, and 120 V5 on the ferry.
  • 320 handhelds.
  • Rider App, Rider Portal and Back-Office Portal.
  • 17 cloud services across two AKS clusters in East US 2, with a warm standby in Central US.
  • Integrations live with Northgate, State DHS, CornerPay, CRT ERP and the RiverCard Bridge to Castellan.

Not deployed yet, by plan:

  • Partner Portal, Apportionment and Keel integration (Phase 4).
  • Customer migration (Phase 3; wave M1 started 2028-03-01).
